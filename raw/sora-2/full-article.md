# Sora 2 is here

OpenAI's flagship video and audio generation model: more physically accurate, realistic, and controllable than prior systems, with synchronized dialogue and sound effects, released alongside a new Sora iOS app.

The original Sora model (February 2024) was, in OpenAI's framing, the "GPT-1 moment for video": the first time video generation started to seem like it was working, with simple behaviors like object permanence emerging from scaling up pre-training compute. Sora 2 is framed as the "GPT-3.5 moment for video," able to do things exceptionally difficult or previously impossible for prior video models: Olympic gymnastics routines, physically accurate backflips on a paddleboard, and complex multi-subject scenes.

Prior video models are described as "overoptimistic," morphing objects or deforming reality to satisfy a prompt (e.g. a missed basketball shot teleporting into the hoop). In Sora 2, a missed shot rebounds off the backboard instead; OpenAI frames the model's "mistakes" as mistakes of an internal physics-obeying agent rather than pure hallucination. The model is a leap forward in controllability, following intricate multi-shot instructions while persisting world state, and can create sophisticated background soundscapes, speech, and sound effects. It can also insert a real person (or animal or object) into a generated environment with an accurate portrayal of appearance and voice, given a short reference video.

## Deployment: the Sora app

OpenAI launched a new social iOS app, "Sora," powered by Sora 2, with a "characters" feature letting users insert themselves or friends into any Sora-generated scene after a one-time video-and-audio identity verification recording. Users can create, remix each other's generations, and browse a customizable feed.

## Launching responsibly

To address doomscrolling, addiction, isolation, and "RL-sloptimized feeds" concerns: natural-language-instructable recommender algorithms, periodic wellbeing check-ins, a feed biased toward people the user follows/interacts with and content likely useful as creation inspiration (not optimized for time-in-feed). Teen protections: default daily generation limits, stricter character permissions, human moderator teams for bullying review, and parental controls (override infinite-scroll limits, disable algorithm personalization, manage DMs) via ChatGPT.

Users control their own character likeness end-to-end: only the user decides who can use their character, and can revoke access or remove any video containing it, including others' drafts, at any time. Monetization plan at launch: an eventual option to pay for extra generations under compute constraints, with no other monetization changes without communication.

## Availability

The Sora iOS app launched first in the U.S. and Canada, with web access via sora.com after invite. Free at launch with generous limits subject to compute constraints; ChatGPT Pro users got access to an experimental higher-quality Sora 2 Pro model on sora.com. API release and Sora 1 Turbo continuity were also planned.
