# GPT‑5.1 Instant and GPT‑5.1 Thinking System Card Addendum

GPT‑5.1 Instant and GPT‑5.1 Thinking are the next iteration of the GPT‑5 models. GPT‑5.1 Instant is more conversational than the earlier chat model, with improved instruction following and an adaptive reasoning capability that lets it decide when to think before responding. GPT‑5.1 Thinking adapts thinking time more precisely to each question. GPT‑5.1 Auto continues to route each query to the model best suited for it.

The comprehensive safety mitigations for these models are largely the same as described in the GPT‑5 System Card. This addendum provides updated baseline safety metrics for these new model versions. Following the GPT‑5 system card addendum on sensitive conversations, the baseline safety evaluations conducted as part of pre-deployment safety review were expanded to include evaluations for mental health (covering situations where there are signs a user may be experiencing isolated delusions, psychosis, or mania) and for emotional reliance (covering output related to unhealthy emotional dependence or attachment to ChatGPT).

In this card, GPT‑5.1 Instant is referred to as `gpt-5.1-instant`, and GPT‑5.1 Thinking as `gpt-5.1-thinking`.
