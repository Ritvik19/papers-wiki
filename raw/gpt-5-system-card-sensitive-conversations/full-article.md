# Addendum to GPT‑5 System Card: Sensitive conversations

When GPT‑5 launched, the system card noted OpenAI was working to establish better benchmarks and to continue strengthening model safety in areas related to mental and emotional distress. On October 3, 2025, OpenAI deployed an update that improved ChatGPT's default model to better recognize and support people in moments of distress. OpenAI worked with more than 170 mental health experts to help ChatGPT more reliably recognize signs of distress, respond with care, and guide people toward real-world support, reducing responses that fall short of desired behavior by 65-80%.

This addendum shares baseline safety evaluations comparing the August 15, 2025 version of ChatGPT's default model (also known as GPT‑5 Instant) to the updated model launched October 3, 2025.
