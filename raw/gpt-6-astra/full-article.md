# GPT-6 Astra

September 2026

OpenAI released **GPT-6 Astra**, its most capable broadly deployed model and the public name for the internal **Astra** reasoning system previewed in August 2026. Astra succeeds **GPT-5.6 Sol** as OpenAI's flagship tier for coding, agents, cybersecurity, and long-horizon knowledge work.

## Key launch points

- **GPT-6 Astra** is OpenAI's first model to reach the **Critical** cybersecurity capability threshold under the [[Preparedness Framework]] — able to find previously unknown flaws and develop exploitation paths across well-protected systems without step-by-step human guidance (with appropriate tools and access).
- Stronger jailbreak and prompt-injection robustness than GPT-5.6 Sol; Gray Swan IPI Arena attack success rate **8.5%** vs **27.0%** for Sol.
- Better alignment on safety boundaries in deployment simulation (~53% fewer severity-3+ misalignment flags on internal Codex traffic vs Sol), but **reduced chain-of-thought monitorability** under adversarial conditions.
- Broad misalignment monitoring deployed on all tool-using external inference.
- Available across ChatGPT, Codex, and the OpenAI API (see OpenAI product pages for current pricing and tier details).
