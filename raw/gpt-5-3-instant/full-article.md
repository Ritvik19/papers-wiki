# GPT‑5.3 Instant: Smoother, more useful everyday conversations

An update to ChatGPT's most-used model that makes everyday conversations more consistently helpful and fluid. GPT‑5.3 Instant delivers more accurate answers, richer and better-contextualized results when searching the web, and reduces unnecessary dead ends, caveats, and overly declarative phrasing that can interrupt the flow of conversation. The update focuses on tone, relevance, and conversational flow, problems that don't always show up in benchmarks but shape whether ChatGPT feels helpful or frustrating, based directly on user feedback.

## Better judgment around refusals and fewer disclaimers

GPT‑5.2 Instant would sometimes refuse questions it should be able to answer safely, or respond in overly cautious or preachy ways around sensitive topics. GPT‑5.3 Instant significantly reduces unnecessary refusals while toning down defensive or moralizing preambles, so useful answers are provided directly with fewer dead ends.

## More useful, well-synthesized answers when using the web

GPT‑5.3 Instant better balances information found online with its own knowledge and reasoning (e.g. contextualizing recent news rather than simply summarizing search results), is less likely to overindex on web results (avoiding long lists of loosely connected links), and surfaces the most important information upfront.

## A smoother, more to-the-point conversational style

GPT‑5.2 Instant's tone could sometimes feel overbearing or make unwarranted assumptions about user intent or emotions. This update has a more focused, natural conversational style, cutting back on unnecessary proclamations, while working to keep ChatGPT's personality consistent across conversations and updates.

## More reliably accurate responses

GPT‑5.3 Instant reduces hallucinations across a wide range of topics, measured with two internal evaluations: one on higher-stakes domains (medicine, law, finance), another on de-identified ChatGPT conversations flagged by users as factual errors. On the higher-stakes evaluation, hallucination rates drop 26.8% with web use and 19.7% without, versus prior models. On the user-feedback evaluation, hallucinations decrease 22.5% with web use and 9.6% without.

## Stronger writing, with more range and texture

GPT‑5.3 Instant is a stronger writing partner: better at resonant, imaginative, immersive prose across fiction, editing, and idea exploration, moving fluidly between practical and expressive writing without losing clarity.

## Limitations

- Non-English languages: response style in some languages (Japanese, Korean) can sound stilted or overly literal; improving tone and naturalness across languages is an ongoing focus.
- Tone: continuing to monitor feedback and expand customization options.

## Availability

Available to all users in ChatGPT starting the release date, and to developers in the API as `gpt-5.3-chat-latest`. Updates to Thinking and Pro followed soon after. GPT‑5.2 Instant remained available for three months for paid users under Legacy Models, retiring June 3, 2026.
