# GPT‑5.5 Instant System Card

GPT‑5.5 Instant is OpenAI's latest Instant model. The comprehensive safety mitigation approach for this model is similar to previous models in this series, but this is the first Instant model treated as **High capability** in both the Cybersecurity and Biological & Chemical Preparedness categories, with appropriate safeguards implemented.

Referred to in this card as `gpt-5.5-instant`. Note there is no model named GPT‑5.4 Instant, so the main baseline is GPT‑5.3 Instant. GPT‑5.5 (the Thinking model) is referred to as GPT‑5.5 Thinking in this card to avoid confusion with the Instant model.
