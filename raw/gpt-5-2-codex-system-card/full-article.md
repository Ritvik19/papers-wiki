# Addendum to GPT‑5.2 System Card: GPT‑5.2‑Codex

GPT‑5.2‑Codex is OpenAI's most advanced agentic coding model yet for complex, real-world software engineering: a version of GPT‑5.2 optimized for agentic coding in Codex, with further improvements on long-horizon work through context compaction, stronger performance on project-scale tasks (refactors, migrations), improved performance in Windows environments, and significantly stronger cybersecurity capabilities.

This system card outlines comprehensive safety measures: model-level mitigations (specialized safety training for harmful tasks and prompt injections) and product-level mitigations (agent sandboxing, configurable network access).

Evaluated under the Preparedness Framework: very capable in cybersecurity but does not reach High capability. OpenAI expects models to cross the High cybersecurity threshold in the near future. Treated as High capability on biology, deployed with the corresponding safeguards used across the GPT‑5 family. Does not reach High capability on AI self-improvement.
