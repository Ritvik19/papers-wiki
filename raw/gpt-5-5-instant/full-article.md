# GPT‑5.5 Instant: smarter, clearer, and more personalized

ChatGPT's default model update: smarter and more accurate, with clearer, more concise answers tailored to the user. Update on June 9, 2026: personalization improvements rolled out to ChatGPT Go & Free (Free tier draws from a reduced set of past chats).

## Smarter, more accurate answers with less to sort through

GPT‑5.5 Instant produced 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims than GPT‑5.3 Instant on high-stakes prompts (medicine, law, finance) in internal evaluations, and reduced inaccurate claims by 37.3% on especially challenging conversations users had flagged for factual errors. Also generally smarter across everyday tasks: analyzing photo/image uploads, STEM questions, and deciding when to use web search.

In a worked math example, GPT‑5.5 Instant recovers from an initial mistake (catching that a proposed solution fails when plugged back into the original equation) and correctly re-derives the quadratic via the quadratic formula, where GPT‑5.3 Instant stops too early and incorrectly concludes "no real solution" instead of revisiting the algebra.

Responses are tighter and more to-the-point without losing substance: in a workplace-advice example, GPT‑5.5 Instant used 30.2% fewer words and 29.2% fewer lines than GPT‑5.3 Instant while keeping the tone informal and practical, framing the issue around boundaries rather than personality, and asking fewer unnecessary follow-up questions.

## More personalized responses and controls

Instant more effectively uses context from past chats, files, and connected Gmail (if enabled), deciding when a response benefits from additional personalization and searching past conversations faster so users repeat themselves less. In a worked example (a tea-place recommendation), GPT‑5.5 Instant references past chats and connected data to give more refined, personalized suggestions than GPT‑5.3 Instant's more generic location-based answer.

OpenAI introduced **memory sources** across all ChatGPT models: visibility over what context (saved memories, past chats) was used to personalize a response, with controls to delete or correct outdated or irrelevant items. Memory sources are not shown to others if a chat is shared; users can delete chats, delete/change saved memories, or use temporary chats that don't use or update memory. Memory sources may not show every factor that shaped an answer (e.g. may show only the most relevant past chats rather than all searched/referenced ones).

## Availability

Rolling out to all ChatGPT users, replacing GPT‑5.3 Instant as the default model, and in the API as `chat-latest`. GPT‑5.3 Instant remained available for three months for paid users via model configuration settings before retirement. Enhanced personalization from past chats/files/Gmail rolled out first to Plus and Pro users on web, then mobile, with plans to expand to Free, Go, Business, and Enterprise. Memory sources rolled out across all ChatGPT consumer plans on web, then mobile. Availability of specific personalization sources may vary by region.
