Introducing Grok 4.5

Jul 8, 2026 · https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5

Grok 4.5 is SpaceXAI's smartest model built for coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work. It's our strongest model ever and was trained alongside Cursor.

Real-world engineering excellence

Grok 4.5 was trained on datasets spanning knowledge in coding, science, engineering, and math. With both intelligent and efficient reasoning, Grok 4.5 excels at real engineering tasks and exceeds comparable leading models at these tasks.

DeepSWE 1.1 (pass@1): Grok 4.5 66.1%, Fable max 64.31%, GPT 5.5 xhigh 62.0%, Grok 4 55.75%, Opus 4.8 max 40.12%. Eval created by Datacurve, run with each model provider's harnesses by Artificial Analysis. Competitor figures from respective developers' published system cards or benchmark leaderboards.

Training Grok 4.5

Grok 4.5 was trained across tens of thousands of NVIDIA GB300 GPUs, with training and stability techniques designed for large-scale runs. Beyond raw token volume, we invested heavily in data filtering and curation: deduplication, quality scoring, and domain-focused selection so that the data mixture stayed high-coverage and high-signal.

We scaled reinforcement learning with a strong focus on per-token intelligence. Our RL training covers hundreds of thousands of tasks, centered on multi-step software engineering and other technical work, with automated and model-based grading. Our stack is built for highly asynchronous training, so agentic rollouts can run for many hours while learning continues across tens of thousands of GPUs.

Built with one prompt

Grok 4.5 is incredibly capable at coding, from challenging Rust and C/C++ tasks to end-to-end app building from prompt to production. Grok 4.5 is highly proficient at creating well-designed, end-to-end functional apps even with minimal specification.

Faster than flash models

Grok 4.5 is served at fast-model speeds of 80 TPS. Combined with twice greater token efficiency than the latest leading models at the same tasks, the model delivers intelligent results more quickly and at far lower costs.

Token efficiency: Grok 4.5 uses 4.2× fewer output tokens than Opus 4.8 (max) on SWE-Bench Pro tasks (avg. output tokens per task).

Excels at Office work

Grok 4.5 is now the default model in Grok Build. Grok Build can build complex Excel models involving web research, multi-sheet formulas, and sticky notes for future reference. In PowerPoint and Word, Grok 4.5 uses native shapes for diagrams, designs slide content, and writes clear prose.

Pricing

Grok 4.5 is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. The model achieves roughly 2× the token efficiency of comparable leading models.

Getting started

Grok 4.5 is available in Grok Build, Cursor on all plans, and the SpaceXAI API console (grok-4.5). Not yet available in the EU; expected mid-July. Free usage for a limited time in Grok Build and Cursor.
