# Pseudolikelihood

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Pseudolikelihood replaces the joint likelihood with a product of conditionals, each predicting one variable given neighbors. It yields tractable objectives for [[Undirected Graphical Models]] when the partition function is hard.

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 18.3 for learning Markov random fields without full partition-function gradients.
