# Compress-Distill

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
A distillation methodology that uses intermediate compressor language models to rewrite and condense verbose long-form reasoning traces into dense, concise rationales (reducing tokens by 80%+) before distilling into compact student models.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained 598: Compress & Distil]] — introduced by Geva et al. (2026).

## Notes
- Outperforms naive truncation and reduces distillation compute requirements.
