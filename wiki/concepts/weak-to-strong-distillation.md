# Weak-to-Strong Distillation

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
The paradigm and methodology of transferring capabilities from a much larger, highly capable teacher language model to a compact, lower-capacity student model using on-policy trajectory generation and distribution alignment.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained 589: Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation]] — systematic study of weak-to-strong on-policy distillation dynamics.

## Notes
- Utilizes mode-seeking reverse KL objectives so that capacity-constrained students concentrate probability mass on executable, high-precision solutions.
