# Directed Graphical Models

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Directed graphical models (Bayesian networks) factorize joint distributions as products of conditional distributions following a DAG structure. They encode causal and hierarchical dependencies among random variables.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Chapter 16.2 (Figure 16.2) develops directed models for structured probabilistic deep learning.

## Notes

Feedforward neural networks can be viewed as directed graphs over stochastic or deterministic units. [[Generative Adversarial Networks]] and directed generative nets (Chapter 20.10) build on this formalism.
