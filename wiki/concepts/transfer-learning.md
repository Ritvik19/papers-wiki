# Transfer Learning

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Transfer learning reuses representations or parameters trained on a source task or dataset to improve performance on a target task with limited data. Fine-tuning pretrained networks is the dominant paradigm in vision and language.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 15.2 covers transfer learning and domain adaptation in the representation-learning chapter.

## Notes

Modern LLMs are extreme transfer learning: pretrain on broad data, fine-tune or align on downstream tasks. The book's greedy layer-wise pretraining (15.1) was an earlier strategy before large-scale pretraining.
