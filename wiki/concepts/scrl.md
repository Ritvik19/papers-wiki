# SCRL

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
Subproblem Curriculum Reinforcement Learning (SCRL); a reinforcement learning framework that decomposes complex reasoning problems into verifiable subproblem DAGs and assigns progress-aware intermediate rewards to guide long-horizon exploration.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained: Subproblem Curriculum Reinforcement Learning (SCRL)]] — introduced by Zhang et al. (2026).

## Notes
- Overcomes the sparse reward bottleneck in multi-step theorem proving and coding.
