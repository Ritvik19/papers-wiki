# Overfitting

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Overfitting occurs when a model fits training data too closely—including noise—and generalizes poorly to new data. In deep learning, high-capacity models can memorize small datasets unless regularized.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 5.2 defines capacity, overfitting, and underfitting; Chapter 7 surveys regularization techniques to combat it.

## Notes

The bias–variance tradeoff (Section 5.4) explains part of the phenomenon; deep nets complicate the classical picture because very large models can still generalize with proper regularization and scale (double descent is outside the book's scope).
