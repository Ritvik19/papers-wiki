# Life Sciences Verification Program

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

The **Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP)** is Anthropic's trusted-access channel for life-sciences professionals to use **Claude Mythos 5.1** with safeguards tuned for professional R&D (while other safeguards remain). Developed in partnership with the US government; first participants enrolled at launch with broader community access planned.
