# Unsupervised Process Reward Models

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
A framework (uPRM) for training step-level Process Reward Models for mathematical reasoning without human step annotations, using joint multi-trajectory LLM scoring and entropy-regularized optimization.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained: Unsupervised Process Reward Models]] — introduced by Kim et al. (2026).

## Notes
- Matches supervised PRMs on ProcessBench and acts as a drop-in dense reward for RLVR.
