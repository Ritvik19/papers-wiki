# Generative Stochastic Networks

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Generative stochastic networks (GSNs) define a Markov chain whose stationary distribution is the data distribution, using learned transition operators that may inject noise. They generalize denoising autoencoders and some Gibbs-style samplers.
