# Noise-Pruned Curriculum Loss

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Noise-pruned curriculum loss (NPCL; Lyu & Tsang, ICLR 2020) is a tighter upper bound on the zero-one loss than hinge loss, with pruning of the highest-loss fraction matching a known label corruption rate $\rho$. Comparable to [[Generalized Cross Entropy]] on noisy CIFAR-10; stronger when noise rate increases.
