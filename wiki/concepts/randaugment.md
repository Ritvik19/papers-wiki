# RandAugment

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

RandAugment (Cubuk et al., 2019) simplifies AutoAugment by searching only a single **magnitude** hyperparameter while uniformly sampling augmentation operations from PIL. Cheap and effective; adopted in [[Unsupervised Data Augmentation]], [[FixMatch]], and [[Noisy Student]].
