# Deep Belief Networks

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Deep belief networks (DBNs) stack [[Restricted Boltzmann Machine]]s and train them greedily layer-wise, then optionally fine-tune. They were among the first successful deep generative pretraining pipelines.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 20.3 describes DBN composition and training.

## Notes

Historical milestone (Hinton et al.) demonstrating depth could help; largely replaced by supervised deep CNNs and modern generative models (GANs, diffusion).
