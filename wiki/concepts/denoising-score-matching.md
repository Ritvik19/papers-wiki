# Denoising Score Matching

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Denoising score matching learns the score of a smoothed data distribution by predicting denoising directions from corrupted samples. It avoids explicit partition functions, connects to [[Denoising Autoencoders]], and forms the theoretical foundation for continuous-time score-based generative models and [[Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models]] (DDPM).
