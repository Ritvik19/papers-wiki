# Unsupervised On-Policy Self-Distillation

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
A label-free post-training method (U-OPSD) where a model generates reasoning traces in thinking mode, derives pseudo-labels via self-consistency voting, and distills the reasoning capability back into its base policy through on-policy alignment without external supervision.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained 595: Unsupervised On-Policy Self-Distillation]] — introduced by Zhang et al. (2026).

## Notes
- Overcomes the need for ground-truth verifiers or external teacher models in reasoning post-training.
