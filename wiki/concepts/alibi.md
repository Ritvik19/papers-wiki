# ALiBi

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
Attention with Linear Biases (Press et al., 2021); a positional encoding mechanism that applies fixed, head-specific linear distance penalties directly to attention logits, enabling zero-shot length extrapolation at inference time.

## Notes
- Head slopes $m$ are geometrically spaced across attention heads.
