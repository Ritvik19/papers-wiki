# Enterprise Frontier Safeguards

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

**Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS)** is Anthropic's architecture for enterprise customers to get **zero data retention–equivalent privacy** while retaining state-of-the-art misuse detection. Customer conversation data stays in **cloud infrastructure controlled by the customer**, not Anthropic; human review defaults to the customer. Developed with 100+ enterprises and cloud partners (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure).
