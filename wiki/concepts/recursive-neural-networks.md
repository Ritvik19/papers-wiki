# Recursive Neural Networks

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Recursive neural networks apply the same weight matrix over tree-structured inputs (e.g. parse trees), combining child representations into parent nodes. They generalize [[Recurrent Neural Networks]] from chains to trees.
