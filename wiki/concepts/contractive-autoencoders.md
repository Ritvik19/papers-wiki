# Contractive Autoencoders

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Contractive autoencoders penalize the Frobenius norm of the encoder Jacobian, encouraging representations insensitive to small input perturbations—learning local tangent structure of the data manifold.
