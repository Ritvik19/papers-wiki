# Model Capacity

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Model capacity is the ability of a model family to fit diverse functions (e.g. via parameter count, depth, width). Too much capacity causes [[Overfitting]]; too little causes underfitting. Regularization reduces effective capacity.
