# Deep Boltzmann Machine

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Deep Boltzmann machines (DBMs) stack multiple layers of hidden units with undirected connections between adjacent layers. They are more expressive than DBNs but harder to train and sample from.

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 20.4 (Figure 20.3 shows bipartite rearrangement).

## Notes

Requires approximate inference during training; research-oriented compared to mainstream practice. Illustrates challenges of [[Partition Function]] and MCMC in deep undirected models.
