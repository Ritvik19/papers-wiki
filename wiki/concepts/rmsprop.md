# RMSProp

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

RMSProp divides gradients by a running root-mean-square of recent squared gradients per parameter, giving adaptive step sizes that help with non-stationary objectives and recurrent nets. It is a precursor to [[Adam]].
