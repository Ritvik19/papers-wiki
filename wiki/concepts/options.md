# Options

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Options (temporal abstraction) are temporally extended actions: a policy over lower-level actions, a termination condition, and an initiation set. An agent choosing among options can solve tasks at multiple time scales—macro-actions like “go to the doorway” instead of primitive motor commands. Sutton & Barto introduce options in the context of semi-Markov decision processes (Chapter 17).

## Notes

Hierarchical RL, skills, and tool-use in agents are modern descendants. Options connect planning horizons in LLM agents (multi-step tool chains) to classical temporal abstraction.
