# Boltzmann Machines

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Boltzmann machines are fully connected undirected energy-based models with binary (or continuous) units. Learning requires sampling from the model distribution; the [[Restricted Boltzmann Machine]] restricts connectivity for tractability.
