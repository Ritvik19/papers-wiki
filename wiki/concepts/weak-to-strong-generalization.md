# Weak-to-Strong Generalization

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
A paradigm in machine learning and alignment where supervision or guidance from weaker models or lower-capacity estimators is used to elicit, steer, or transfer capabilities to or from stronger foundation models.
