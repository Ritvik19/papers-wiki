# Colossus

**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

**Colossus** is xAI's GPU training supercluster used to train Grok 3 onward at unprecedented scale. The Grok 3 announcement cites **10×** the compute of xAI's prior state-of-the-art training runs; Grok 4 uses a **200,000-GPU** cluster to run reinforcement-learning training that refines reasoning at pretraining scale, with reported **6×** compute-efficiency gains over Grok 3 Reasoning infrastructure.

## Appearances

- [[Grok Models#Grok 3 Beta (Feb 2025)]] — Grok 3 trained on Colossus at 10× prior compute; 1M context, Think mode RL.
- [[Grok Models#Grok 4 (Jul 2025)]] — 200K-GPU Colossus runs RL at pretraining scale for Grok 4 reasoning.
- [[Grok Models#Grok 4.5 (Jul 2026)]] — Grok 4.5 trained across tens of thousands of NVIDIA GB300 GPUs with async RL at hundreds of thousands of tasks.
- [[xAI]] — org operating Colossus as core training infrastructure.

## Notes

- Distinct from other "Colossus" names in ML literature; here it refers specifically to xAI's cluster as named in official Grok posts.
