# Fairwind Program

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

The **Fairwind Program** is Google's trusted-access channel for **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber**, providing prioritized access to defender-focused cybersecurity capabilities for government authorities, critical infrastructure operators, and software maintainers. It supersedes the limited CodeMender pilot model for broad defender deployment of Flash Cyber variants.
