# TREK

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
Teacher-Routed Exploration via Forward KL (TREK); an exploration framework for reasoning language models that detects student-unreachable tasks, routes them to a teacher for Forward KL proposal learning, and switches to on-policy refinement once reachability is established.
