# Recursive Self-Improvement

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

**Recursive self-improvement (RSI)** is a feedback loop in which an AI system uses its current capabilities to improve the cognitive machinery that produces future capabilities. I. J. Good (1965) coined the ultraintelligent-machine idea; Yudkowsky (2008) emphasized AI improving its own intelligence-producing machinery.

## Appearances

- [[Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement]] — frames modern RSI as improving training pipelines and deployment systems (harnesses), not only rewriting weights.

## Notes

Near-term RSI is unlikely to start with direct weight self-editing. Harness engineering may evolve into meta-methodology while stronger models prevent over-engineered harnesses. Many harness tricks may eventually internalize into core model behavior, but tool/context interfaces likely remain.
