# Maximum Likelihood Estimation

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Maximum likelihood estimation (MLE) chooses parameters that maximize the probability of observed data under a parametric model. Cross-entropy loss for classification is equivalent to MLE under a softmax/categorical model.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 5.5 derives MLE; Chapter 6 connects it to supervised training of neural networks.

## Notes

MLE can overfit without regularization. The book contrasts MLE with [[Bayesian Statistics]] (Section 5.6) for incorporating parameter uncertainty.
