# Bias-Variance Tradeoff

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

The bias–variance tradeoff decomposes generalization error into bias (systematic underfitting), variance (sensitivity to training set sampling), and irreducible noise. Increasing model capacity typically reduces bias but increases variance.

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 5.4 covers estimators, bias, variance, and their role in model selection and regularization.

## Notes

Deep learning sometimes violates the classical U-shaped risk curve at scale (interpolating models can still generalize). The book presents the classical framework that motivates regularization and validation.
