# TinyGSM

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept #entity

## Overview
A 1.3B parameter mathematical reasoning model by Microsoft Research trained on 12.3M programmatically verified synthetic word problems, achieving 81.5% on GSM8K.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained: TinyGSM]] — technical report (arXiv:2312.09241).

## Notes
- Proved that synthetic data quality rather than raw model scale determines grade-school math reasoning capability.
