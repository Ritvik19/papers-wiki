# Greedy Layer-Wise Pretraining

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Greedy layer-wise pretraining trains deep networks one layer at a time as unsupervised feature learners (e.g. autoencoders or RBMs), then fine-tunes end-to-end. It was used to initialize very deep nets when end-to-end training was difficult.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Sections 8.7 and 15.1 (Figure 8.7) describe supervised and unsupervised greedy pretraining strategies.

## Notes

Largely superseded by better initialization, normalization, and large-scale end-to-end training—but historically important for understanding depth and optimization challenges.
