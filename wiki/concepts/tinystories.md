# TinyStories

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept #entity

## Overview
A synthetic dataset and experimental framework created by Microsoft Research (Eldan & Li, 2023) showing that tiny transformers (1M–33M parameters) can achieve grammatical fluency and narrative coherence when trained on clean, concept-constrained synthetic text.

- [[Papers Explained: TinyStories]] — foundational paper (arXiv:2305.07759).

## Notes
- Laid the groundwork for the Phi model family and synthetic pretraining paradigms.
