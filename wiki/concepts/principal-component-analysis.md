# Principal Component Analysis

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Principal component analysis (PCA) finds orthogonal directions of maximum variance in data, projecting inputs onto a lower-dimensional subspace. It is a linear dimensionality reduction and factor model baseline.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 2.12 (linear algebra example) and Chapter 13 (linear factor models) treat PCA and its probabilistic interpretation.

## Notes

PCA precedes deep [[Representation Learning]]; autoencoders with linear activations relate to PCA. Used for visualization, preprocessing, and understanding manifolds.
