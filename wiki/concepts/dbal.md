# DBAL

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Deep Bayesian Active Learning (DBAL; Gal et al., 2017) uses [[MC Dropout]] to approximate Bayesian neural networks for active learning acquisition, measuring epistemic uncertainty via stochastic forward passes at inference time.
