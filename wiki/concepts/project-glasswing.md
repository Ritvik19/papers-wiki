# Project Glasswing

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

**Project Glasswing** is an Anthropic-led initiative (announced April 2026) that brings together major technology companies, financial institutions, and security vendors to secure critically important software using advanced AI cyber-defense capabilities. **Claude Mythos Preview** and **Claude Mythos 5** deploy through Glasswing to vetted cyberdefenders and infrastructure providers.

## Appearances

- [[Claude Models]] — Mythos 5 launches as a Glasswing upgrade from Mythos Preview (June 2026).
- [[Anthropic]] — initiative partner and Mythos-class model provider.

## Notes

- Partners include AWS, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, JPMorganChase, Linux Foundation, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Palo Alto Networks, and others (per Anthropic announcements).
- By May 2026, ~50 partners used Mythos Preview to find 10k+ high/critical vulnerabilities; expansion to ~150 organizations in 15+ countries announced June 2026.
- Mythos 5 remains restricted to trusted-access programs; general users receive **Claude Fable 5** (same base model with cyber/biology safeguards).
