# Markov Chain Monte Carlo

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) approximates expectations under a target distribution by sampling from a Markov chain whose stationary distribution is the target. [[Gibbs Sampling]] is a common special case.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Chapter 17 covers sampling, MCMC, mixing, and multimodal distributions (Figure 17.1).

## Notes

Used for learning and inference in undirected models; too slow for large-scale deep learning training today but foundational for probabilistic ML. Distinct from RL [[Monte Carlo Methods]] (return averaging).
