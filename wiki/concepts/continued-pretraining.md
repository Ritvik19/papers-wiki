# Continued Pretraining

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Domain-adaptive pretraining from a base checkpoint on new corpora before task-specific SFT. Unsloth documents CPT workflows with memory-optimized kernels and QLoRA for extending models to new domains.
