# Process Reward Models

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
Process Reward Models (PRMs) are verifier models that evaluate and assign step-by-step credit or correctness scores to individual intermediate reasoning steps in a multi-step solution trajectory, contrasting with outcome reward models (ORMs) that score only terminal answers.
