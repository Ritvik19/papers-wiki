# Passive Skill Distillation

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview
A lightweight post-training technique (PSD) that transfers specialized procedural and tool-use skills from offline interaction logs into compact models without requiring active online rollouts or interactive teacher queries.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained: Passive Skill Distillation]] — introduced by Rastogi et al. (2026).

## Notes
- Highly compute-efficient ($10\times$ less training compute than online RL/KD).
