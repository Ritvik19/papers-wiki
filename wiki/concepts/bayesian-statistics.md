# Bayesian Statistics

**Type**: concept  
**Tags**: #concept

## Overview

Bayesian statistics treats parameters as random variables with priors, updating beliefs via Bayes' rule after observing data. It provides uncertainty estimates and principled regularization via priors.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — Section 5.6 contrasts Bayesian approaches with [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]]; later chapters connect to approximate inference (Chapters 19–20).

## Notes

Exact Bayesian inference is intractable for large neural nets; variational methods and MCMC approximations bridge theory and practice. Modern Bayesian deep learning extends these ideas.
