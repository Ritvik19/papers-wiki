## Summary

Google's Keyword blog peels back the origin of **Nano Banana**, one of [[Google DeepMind]]'s most viral image models. The technical name was always **Gemini 2.5 Flash Image**, but the team needed a public **codename for [LMArena](https://lmarena.ai/)** — an anonymous pairwise-comparison arena where unreleased models gather early human feedback before launch.

At **2:30 a.m.** the night before submission, Product Manager **Naina Raisinghani** proposed "Nano Banana" as something deliberately nonsensical. The name fuses her personal nicknames — friends call her "Naina Banana" and "Nano" (short stature + love of computers) — and fit the Flash model tier. Nano Banana debuted on LMArena in early August; users were stunned by editing fidelity (likeness preservation, multi-image compositing) and the meme-worthy name drove social discourse. After weeks of speculation, Raisinghani teased Google's authorship on X; official launch made Nano Banana the top-rated image editing model globally.

The brand stuck despite the formal Gemini name: yellow run buttons in AI Studio, banana emoji on Gemini's "Create image" chip, limited-edition swag. Gemini 3 Pro Image inherited **Nano Banana Pro**. Success factors cited: global day-one availability (consumer and developer) and culturally viral prompt trends (figurines in Thailand, saree edits in India).

## Key Claims

- Technical name: **Gemini 2.5 Flash Image**; public codename **Nano Banana** invented for LMArena pre-release testing.
- Codename coined by PM Naina Raisinghani at 2:30 a.m., combining nicknames "Naina Banana" + "Nano."
- LMArena uses blind pairwise voting; codenames hide model identity during development evaluation.
- Viral LMArena reception + humorous name fueled social media discourse before Google confirmed authorship.
- At official launch, Nano Banana became top-rated image editing model worldwide.
- Brand persisted in product UI (yellow buttons, banana emoji) while technical name remained Gemini 2.5 Flash Image.
- Success attributed to simultaneous global rollout and culturally specific viral editing trends.
- Successor branding: Gemini 3 Pro Image → **Nano Banana Pro**; team embraced banana emoji culture.
