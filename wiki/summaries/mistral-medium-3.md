## Summary

Mistral AI announces **Mistral Medium 3**, an enterprise-class model positioned between open-weight Small/Large releases and fully proprietary frontier systems. The blog frames it as a new efficiency tier: **SOTA performance at ~8× lower cost**, simpler deployability (self-hosted on **four GPUs and above**), and strong professional use cases—especially **coding, STEM, and multimodal understanding**.

Benchmark claims place Medium 3 at **≥90% of Claude Sonnet 3.7** across the board at **$0.40/M input and $2/M output tokens**. It reportedly surpasses **Llama 4 Maverick** and **Cohere Command A** on performance while beating **DeepSeek v3** on pricing for both API and self-deployed setups. Third-party human evaluations emphasize coding strength vs. much larger competitors.

Enterprise features include **hybrid/on-prem/in-VPC deployment**, **custom post-training**, and integration into enterprise tools via Mistral applied AI solutions (continuous pretraining, full fine-tuning, knowledge-base blending). Beta customers span financial services, energy, and healthcare. API availability: **Mistral La Plateforme**, **Amazon SageMaker**; coming to IBM WatsonX, NVIDIA NIM, Azure AI Foundry, and Google Cloud Vertex.

## Key Claims

- Frontier-class performance at ~8× lower cost; deployable on 4+ GPUs in self-hosted environments.
- ≥90% of Claude Sonnet 3.7 on benchmarks at $0.40 input / $2 output per M tokens.
- Outperforms Llama 4 Maverick and Cohere Command A; beats DeepSeek v3 on cost (API and self-hosted).
- Leads in coding, STEM, and multimodal understanding; strong third-party human evals in coding.
- Enterprise path: hybrid/on-prem, custom post-training, knowledge-base integration, domain-specific workflows.
- Available on La Plateforme and Amazon SageMaker; broader cloud rollout imminent.
