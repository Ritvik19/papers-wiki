# Z.ai

**Type**: org
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Z.ai (formerly Zhipu AI) is a Chinese AI lab developing the GLM family of open-weight large language models, focused on long-horizon agentic coding capability. GLM-5.2 is the flagship model as of this ingest, emphasizing efficient long-context serving and agentic RL infrastructure (`slime`).

## Appearances

- [[GLM-5.2: Built for Long-Horizon Tasks]] — 753B flagship model; IndexShare (shared DSA indexer every 4 layers, 2.9x FLOPs reduction at 1M context), improved MTP speculative decoding (+20% acceptance length), critic-based PPO for long-horizon RL, and an online anti-reward-hacking module for coding RL.
- [[The Future of the Global Open-Source AI Ecosystem: From DeepSeek to AI+]] — cited alongside Moonshot and MiniMax as a startup with rapid post-R1 open-source momentum.

## Notes

GLM-5.2 is released under an MIT license. Z.ai also operates a commercial "GLM Coding Plan" subscription and a desktop coding agent, ZCode.
