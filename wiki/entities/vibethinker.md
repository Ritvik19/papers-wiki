# VibeThinker

**Type**: tool  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**VibeThinker** is a family of compact dense reasoning models (1.5B and 3B) trained with the **Spectrum-to-Signal Principle**: diversity-maximizing SFT followed by MaxEnt-Guided Policy Optimization (MGPO) RL on verifiable math, code, and STEM tasks.

## Appearances

- [[Papers Explained 584: VibeThinker-1.5B]] — SSP + diversity distillation + MGPO on Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B.
- [[Papers Explained 585: VibeThinker-3B]] — curriculum SFT, multi-domain RL, offline self-distillation on Qwen2.5-Coder-3B.

## Notes

VibeThinker-3B achieves frontier-competitive competition math/coding at 3B scale; GPQA knowledge remains a weakness vs large models.
