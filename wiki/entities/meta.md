# Meta

**Type**: org
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Meta (formerly Facebook) is the developer of the Llama family of open-weight large language models, alongside Llama Guard safety classifiers and multimodal research. Llama 4 (Maverick and Scout) marked Meta's shift to Mixture-of-Experts architectures and native multimodality for its flagship open releases.

## Appearances

- [[Welcome Llama 4 Maverick & Scout on Hugging Face]] — launch of Llama 4 Maverick (~400B/17B active) and Scout (~109B/17B active), both MoE, natively multimodal, with iRoPE long-context architecture (NoPE + chunked RoPE + attention temperature tuning) enabling 1M/10M context windows respectively.
- [[Unsloth Model Support 2024]] — Llama 3/3.1/3.2 Vision/3.3 QLoRA support.
- [[Unsloth Model Support 2025]] — Llama 4 QLoRA recipes.

## Notes

Llama 4 Maverick was co-distilled from a larger unreleased model referred to as "Llama Behemoth."
