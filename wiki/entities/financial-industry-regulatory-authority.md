# Financial Industry Regulatory Authority

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

The **Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA)** is a non-governmental, self-regulatory organization (SRO) authorized by the United States Congress to regulate member brokerage firms and exchange markets under Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) oversight. It operates via industry funding, mandatory membership for market participants, and enforceable compliance standards.

## Appearances

- [[A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age]] — Demis Hassabis cites FINRA as the foundational institutional blueprint for a proposed federally overseen, public-private AI Standards Body.
- [[Standards Body for Frontier AI]] — Governed as a self-regulatory organization (SRO) modelled after FINRA's public-private structure.

## Notes

The FINRA model is referenced in AI governance debates because it combines industry-scale technical funding and operational agility with statutory federal enforcement authority, exempting small market participants while mandating compliance for core institutions.
