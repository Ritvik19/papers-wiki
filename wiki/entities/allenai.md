# AllenAI

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

The Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence (AllenAI) is an independent, non-profit research institute founded in 2014 by Microsoft co-founder Paul Allen. It conducts high-impact AI research and engineering in the public interest, championing open science, open weights, and open datasets. The institute is particularly famous for its work in natural language processing (NLP), reasoning systems, and document AI, culminating in major open-weight language model families like OLMo.

- [[Papers Explained: OLMo 3]] — primary creator of OLMo 3, Dolma 3 Mix pretraining data, and the OlmoRL reinforcement learning engine.
- [[Papers Explained 284 - OLMo 2]] — creator of OLMo 2, the predecessor base models.
- [[Papers Explained 479 - olmOCR]] — developer of the olmOCR pipeline used to digitize hundreds of millions of scientific PDF pages.
- [[Papers Explained 98 - OLMo]] — developer of the original open-source OLMo framework, model, and data tools.
- [[Papers Explained 270 - OLMoE]] — developer of OLMoE, a highly efficient sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture.

## Notes

AllenAI is one of the primary drivers of "fully-open" AI research, releasing not just weights, but also raw pretraining data corpora (Dolma), evaluation toolkits, and intermediate model checkpoints to allow researchers to study training dynamics.
