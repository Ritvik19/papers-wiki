# Arcee AI

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Arcee AI is an AI company focused on building and deploying efficient language models optimized for enterprise use cases. They are known for the AFM (Arcee Foundation Models) dense model family and the Arcee Trinity sparse MoE model family.

## Appearances

- [[Papers Explained 426 - Arcee Foundation Models]] — AFM-4.5B, Arcee's initial dense foundation model family targeting enterprise performance at edge-compatible sizes.
- [[Papers Explained: Arcee Trinity]] — Trinity Nano/Mini/Large, Arcee's sparse MoE model family (6B–400B total parameters, 1B–13B active per token).

## Notes

- Arcee AI partnered with [[DatologyAI]] for pretraining data curation on both the AFM and Trinity model families.
- Trinity Large is the first Arcee model trained with the Muon optimizer and the first to use the SMEBU load-balancing strategy.
