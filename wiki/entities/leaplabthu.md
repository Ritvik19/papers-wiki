**Type**: org

## Overview

**LeapLabTHU** (Laboratory for Efficient and Accessible Programming / Deep Learning at Tsinghua University) is an AI and machine learning research group at Tsinghua University led by Prof. Gao Huang. The lab focuses on efficient deep learning architectures, scalable inference, post-training paradigms, and reasoning in large language models.
