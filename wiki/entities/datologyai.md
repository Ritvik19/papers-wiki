# DatologyAI

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

DatologyAI is a data curation company specializing in large-scale pretraining dataset construction for foundation models. Their pipeline integrates model-based quality filtering, embedding-based curation, target distribution matching, source mixing, and synthetic data generation.

## Appearances

- [[Papers Explained 426 - Arcee Foundation Models]] — curated 6.58 trillion tokens for the AFM-4.5B pretraining dataset.
- [[Papers Explained: Arcee Trinity]] — curated all pretraining data for Trinity Nano/Mini (10T token mix) and Trinity Large (17T tokens from a 20T mix including 8T+ synthetic tokens).

## Notes

- DatologyAI's curation approach targets general English, programming, STEM, reasoning, and multilingual skills.
- For Trinity Large, they generated over 8 trillion tokens of synthetic data: 6.5T tokens of synthetic web content (format transformation, style modification, content restructuring), ~1T tokens of synthetic multilingual data, and 800B tokens of synthetic code.
