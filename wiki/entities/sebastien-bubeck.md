# Sebastien Bubeck

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Sébastien Bubeck is a prominent mathematician and AI researcher at [[OpenAI]], formerly Vice President of AI and Distinguished Scientist at [[Microsoft]] (Microsoft Research). His research spans machine learning theory, convex optimization, multi-armed bandits, the physics of large language models, small high-capability models (e.g., the Phi model series), and the empirical assessment of reasoning in frontier AI models.

## Appearances

- [[A Single Question to Track Progress from o3 to GPT-5.6 and Beyond]] — Author of the article tracking model reasoning evolution from o3 to [[GPT-5.6]] via the open problem of self-contracted gradient flows in convex optimization.
- [[Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement]] — Referenced for research on early science acceleration with frontier LLMs and empirical failure patterns ("p-hacking and eureka-ing") in autonomous research.

## Notes

- Bubeck has pioneered both theoretical machine learning (authoring standard texts on bandit algorithms and convex optimization geometry) and foundational empirical work on LLMs (notably leading Microsoft's *Sparks of AGI* research with GPT-4 in 2023 and the Phi series).
- Uses specific open research-level mathematical problems with clean formulations (e.g., length of self-contracted curves in the unit ball) as invariant probes of AI reasoning capability across model generations.
