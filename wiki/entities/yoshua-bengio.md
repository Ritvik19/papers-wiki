# Yoshua Bengio

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Yoshua Bengio is a computer scientist and Turing Award laureate (2018, with Hinton and LeCun) recognized as a founder of modern deep learning. He co-authored *Deep Learning* (MIT Press, 2016) and has led research on neural language models, representation learning, and causality.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — co-author of the canonical deep learning textbook ingested into this wiki.
- [[Learning Word Embedding]] — [[Hierarchical Softmax]] for neural language models (Morin & Bengio, AISTATS 2005); reduces Word2Vec training from O(V) to O(log V) per sample.

## Notes

Bengio's lab at Mila (Montreal) produced influential work on sequence modeling, attention precursors, and unsupervised representation learning that shaped the book's research-oriented Part III.
