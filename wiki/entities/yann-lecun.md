# Yann LeCun

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Yann LeCun is a pioneering computer scientist, Turing Award laureate, and VP & Chief AI Scientist at Meta (FAIR). He is widely recognized for his foundational contributions to deep learning, including the development of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and the promotion of self-supervised learning as a crucial paradigm for training intelligent systems.

## Appearances

- [[Self-Supervised Representation Learning]] — His conceptual overview diagram of constructing self-supervised tasks from masked inputs serves as the foundational framing for the pretext objectives.

## Technical highlights in Weng series

- **Self-Supervised Pretext Tasks**: Popularized the framing of self-supervised learning as a process of "filling in the blanks" (masking/hiding portions of inputs and training networks to predict them), which enables learning powerful representations from raw data without manual annotations.
