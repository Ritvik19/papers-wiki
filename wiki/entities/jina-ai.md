# Jina AI

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Jina AI builds embedding and retrieval models for search, RAG, and multimodal applications. The **Jina Embeddings** line spans text-only and omni (text/image/audio/video) encoders; **GELATO** (Locked Aligned TOwers) extends v5 Text with frozen modality encoders and trainable projectors.
