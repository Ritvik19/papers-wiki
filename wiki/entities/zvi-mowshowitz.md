# Zvi Mowshowitz

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**Zvi Mowshowitz** is an AI safety researcher, writer, and commentator, widely known for his comprehensive analytical blog *Don't Worry About the Vase* and weekly syntheses of AI developments, policy, alignment research, and frontier model governance. Mowshowitz focuses on evaluating AI safety commitments, Preparedness Frameworks, Responsible Scaling Policies (RSPs), systemic existential risk, and corporate safety accountability across major AI developers.

## Appearances

- [[Implications of Large-Scale Test-Time Compute]] — quoted regarding his public critique of Google DeepMind's position that the runtime performance improvements in [[Gemini 3 Deep Think]] did not introduce additional misuse risk and thus did not require a dedicated safety model card.
