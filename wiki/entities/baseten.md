# Baseten

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Baseten is an inference platform company that provides production infrastructure for serving generative AI models. It powers mission-critical inference for AI-native companies including Cursor, Clay, Gamma, and Mercor.

## Appearances

- [[Inference Engineering]] — Baseten is the publisher and the company where author Philip Kiely spent four years developing inference expertise. The book draws on Baseten's engineering team interviews and customer experience.

## Notes

Baseten Books published *Inference Engineering* in 2026 (ISBN: 979-8-9943597-2-3). The company's platform spans runtime optimization, multi-cloud infrastructure, and developer tooling — the three layers of the inference stack described in the book. Section 7.6 specifically covers production inference with Baseten.
