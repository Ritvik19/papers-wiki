# Richard S. Sutton

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Richard S. Sutton is a computer scientist and one of the founders of modern reinforcement learning. With Andrew G. Barto he co-authored *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* (MIT Press), the field's standard textbook. His research introduced temporal-difference learning, policy gradient methods, and many algorithms now standard in RL curricula and applications.

## Appearances

- [[Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction]] — co-author of the canonical RL textbook ingested into this wiki.

## Notes

Sutton's work at the University of Alberta and (formerly) DeepMind established the theoretical and algorithmic core that later deep RL systems (DQN, AlphaGo, modern LLM RL) extend. The second edition (2018) adds deep learning, AlphaGo, and updated neuroscience connections.
