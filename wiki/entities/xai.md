# xAI

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**xAI** is an AI company founded by Elon Musk, building the **Grok** assistant integrated with 𝕏 (real-time social knowledge), grok.com, and mobile apps. The lab trains frontier models on **Colossus**, a large GPU supercluster (200K accelerators for Grok 4-era RL), using a JAX + Rust + Kubernetes stack. Product lines span consumer Grok tiers (Premium, Premium+, SuperGrok, SuperGrok Heavy), enterprise and developer **xAI API** (text, voice, imagine, agent tools), and deployments in **Tesla** vehicles and **Starlink** customer support.
