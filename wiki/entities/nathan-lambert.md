# Nathan Lambert

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Nathan Lambert is an ML researcher and educator known for work on reinforcement learning from human feedback and open post-training documentation. He authored the textbook *Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback* ([arXiv 2504.12501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12501), [rlhfbook.com](https://rlhfbook.com)).

## Appearances

- [[Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback]] — author of the open RLHF textbook ingested into this wiki.

## Notes

The book synthesizes industrial RLHF practice (InstructGPT, Tülu, DeepSeek R1 recipes) for researchers and practitioners entering post-training roles.
