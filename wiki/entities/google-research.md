# Google Research

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**Google Research** is Google's research publication and blog brand for advances across machine learning, systems, and applied science. It is distinct from [[DeepMind]] product and model releases, though both sit under Alphabet and often share infrastructure.
