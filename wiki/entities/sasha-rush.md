# Sasha Rush

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Alexander "Sasha" Rush is a machine learning researcher and professor at Cornell University, previously at Harvard. He is known for work on structured prediction, efficient transformers, and language model training methods. He is also a co-founder of Hugging Face's research efforts and a contributor to open-source ML tooling.

## Appearances

- [[Sasha Rush Explains Targeted On-Policy Self-Distillation]] — gives an impromptu lecture to Dwarkesh Patel on sequence knowledge distillation, on-policy distillation, and on-policy self-distillation, explaining how Cursor used targeted textual feedback for Composer 2.5 training.

## Notes

- In the video, Rush provides the clearest public explanation of how Cursor's targeted self-distillation works: same-token re-scoring under injected hints, no new decode.
- His tennis analogy (watching Nadal vs. Nadal correcting your swing) is a memorable intuition for off-policy vs. on-policy teacher signal.
