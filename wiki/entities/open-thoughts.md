# OpenThoughts

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**OpenThoughts** (published under the `open-thoughts` organization on Hugging Face) is an open-science AI research initiative dedicated to creating open-source data curation pipelines, datasets, and training recipes for reasoning and agentic language models.

## Appearances

- [[Papers Explained 394 - OpenThoughts]] — introduces the foundational OpenThoughts open reasoning dataset and task filtering recipes.
- [[Papers Explained 468 - NaturalThoughts]] — naturalistic reasoning trace collection and fine-tuning.
- [[Papers Explained 587: OpenThoughts Agent]] — introduces the OpenThoughts-Agent (OT-Agent) 100K data curation pipeline, systematic ablation suite across 95 task strategies, and OpenThinker-Agent-v1 (fine-tuned Qwen3-32B).
- [[Open R1: Update #1]] — Hugging Face's Open-R1 project integrating OpenThoughts reasoning data for open-source R1 reproductions.

## Notes

- OpenThoughts emphasizes fully open and reproducible data curation recipes, publishing not just final datasets but the systematic ablation methodologies (task mixing, LLM difficulty filtering, teacher selection, and RL data recipes) that produce them.
