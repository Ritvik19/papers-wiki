# Sebastian Raschka

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**Sebastian Raschka, PhD** is an ML educator and author who writes the *Ahead of AI* newsletter (magazine.sebastianraschka.com) and publishes hands-on books including *Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)* and *Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch)*. He maintains open-source teaching implementations such as the [[Mini Coding Agent]].

## Appearances

- [[Understanding the 4 Main Approaches to LLM Evaluation (From Scratch)]] — October 2025 overview of multiple-choice, verifier, leaderboard, and LLM-judge evaluation with from-scratch code (MMLU, Elo, Ollama).
- [[Understanding and Coding the KV Cache in LLMs from Scratch]] — June 2025 tutorial on KV cache concepts and from-scratch PyTorch implementation for the GPT model in his LLM book.
- [[Components of A Coding Agent]] — April 2026 reference article on coding-agent and coding-harness design, illustrated with Mini Coding Agent.
- [[Inkling]] — July 2026 third-party architecture and benchmark notes on Thinking Machines' 975B MoE (relative attention, SConv, SWA-heavy design).
- [[A Visual Guide to Attention Variants in Modern LLMs]] — March 2026 visual reference on MHA, GQA, MLA, SWA, DSA, gated attention, and hybrid stacks; accompanies the public LLM architecture gallery.
- [[Beyond Standard LLMs]] — November 2025 survey of linear attention hybrids, text diffusion LLMs, code world models, and small recursive transformers beyond standard decoder transformers.
- [[Controlling Reasoning Effort in LLMs]] — July 2026 survey of how reasoning models learn low/medium/high effort modes via RLVR, SFT, and open-weight recipes (DeepSeek V4, Nemotron 3 Ultra, Kimi K2.5 Toggle, GLM-5, Qwen3, Inkling).
- [[How Claude Watermarks AI-Generated Text]] — August 2026 lecture and 52-slide breakdown of token sampling, secret-key PRNG seeding, tournament sampling, watermark detection, and evasion dynamics.
- [[Papers Explained 145 - LoRA]] — LoRA method appears in corpus via paper explainers referencing his educational materials.
