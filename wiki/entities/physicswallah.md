# PhysicsWallah

**Type**: org

## Overview

PhysicsWallah (PW) is an Indian ed-tech company. Its AI lab released **Aryabhata 2**, a 20B open STEM reasoning model post-trained from GPT-OSS-20B on verified JEE/NEET-style question banks.
