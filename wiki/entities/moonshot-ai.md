# Moonshot AI

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Moonshot AI is the company behind the Kimi family of large language models. Cursor's Composer 2 and Composer 2.5 both build on Moonshot's open Kimi K2.5 checkpoint as their base model.

## Appearances

- [[Introducing Composer 2.5]] — Kimi K2.5 named as the shared open checkpoint underlying Composer 2.5.
- [[Papers Explained - Composer 2]] — Kimi K2.5 selected over GLM-5 and DeepSeek V3.2 after coding-knowledge, state-tracking, and codebase-perplexity evaluations.

## Notes

- Composer training adds continued pretraining and large-scale RL on top of Kimi K2.5 rather than training the base from scratch.
