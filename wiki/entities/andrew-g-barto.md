# Andrew G. Barto

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Andrew G. Barto is a computer scientist and co-founder of the reinforcement learning field. With Richard S. Sutton he co-authored *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*. His work spans adaptive systems, neuroscience connections to RL (including dopamine reward prediction error), and the computational foundations of learning from reward.

## Appearances

- [[Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction]] — co-author of the canonical RL textbook ingested into this wiki.

## Notes

Barto's contributions include early work on associative search and the intellectual bridge between RL algorithms and animal learning models summarized in the book's psychology and neuroscience chapters (14–15).
