# Tinker

**Type**: tool  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Tinker is [[Thinking Machines Lab]]'s managed platform for fine-tuning and post-training open-weight models. It exposes training APIs, a cookbook of recipes (supervised, distillation, RL), a developer Playground for chatting with base models, and deployment partnerships for checkpoints trained on the platform.

## Appearances

- [[Inkling]] — primary customization surface for Inkling (64K/256K context at launch, limited-time discount); Inkling Playground with agentic web search; self-finetune demo; cookbook updated with audio recipes; partners for deploying Tinker checkpoints (Together AI, Fireworks, Modal, Databricks, Baseten, and open inference stacks).
- [[Inkling-Small]] — released with Tinker fine-tuning support and text/image/audio Playground access.
- [[LoRA Without Regret]] — TML's broader post-training research agenda that Tinker productizes.
