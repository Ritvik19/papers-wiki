# Andrej Karpathy

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**Andrej Karpathy** is an AI researcher, educator, and engineer, formerly Senior Director of AI at Tesla and a founding research scientist at OpenAI. Karpathy is widely known for creating popular open-source educational projects and deep learning architectures (such as `nanoGPT`, `micrograd`, and `llama2.c`), developing curriculum on neural networks, and conducting autonomous research experiments on LLM optimization and training dynamics.

## Appearances

- [[Implications of Large-Scale Test-Time Compute]] — creator of the `autoresearch` experiment autotuning nanoGPT across 276+ iterations, cited by [[Noam Brown]] as empirical proof that model capability scaling does not prematurely plateau over long experimental horizons.
- [[Policy Gradients]] — author of the foundational "Deep Reinforcement Learning: Pong from Pixels" tutorial.
- [[LSTM]] — creator of `char-rnn` and visualizations of recurrent network hidden activations.
- [[nanovlm|nanoVLM]] — reference implementation modeled after Karpathy's nanoGPT.

## Notes

- His `autoresearch` project explores autonomous AI agents driving the iterative design, hyperparameter tuning, and architecture validation of neural networks in an open-ended feedback loop.
