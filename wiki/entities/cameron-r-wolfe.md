# Cameron R. Wolfe

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Cameron R. Wolfe is an artificial intelligence researcher, author, and practitioner. He serves as the Director of AI at Rebuy and is the creator of "Deep Learning Focus", a popular newsletter providing in-depth technical analysis and comprehensive summaries of state-of-the-art AI research. His work focus includes reinforcement learning, alignment, post-training paradigms (such as supervised fine-tuning, DPO, and GRPO), and model compression techniques.

## Appearances

- [[GRPO++: Tricks for Making RL Actually Work]] — author of the original technical analysis and compilation of GRPO variants and best practices (Dr. GRPO, DAPO, GSPO, CISPO) for reasoning model training.

## Notes

Wolfe's writing is highly regarded in the research community for its balance of high-level algorithmic intuition, clear mathematics, and comparative diagrams. His compilation on GRPO serves as a key reference for training reasoning models efficiently.
