# Maxime Labonne

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**Maxime Labonne** (*mlabonne* on Hugging Face) is an ML educator and practitioner known for the LLM Course, model-merging tutorials, and popularizing **abliteration** for removing refusal behavior from instruction-tuned LLMs without retraining.

## Appearances

- [[Uncensor any LLM with abliteration]] — step-by-step abliteration tutorial; Daredevil-8B → NeuralDaredevil-8B-abliterated pipeline with DPO healing.

## Notes

Maintains datasets (`mlabonne/harmful_behaviors`, `mlabonne/harmless_alpaca`, `mlabonne/orpo-dpo-mix-40k`) and collections of abliterated models. Later recommended AutoAbliteration/Heretic for easier replication.
