# Anthropic

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

**Anthropic** is an AI safety and research company (founded 2021; co-founders include Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei) best known for the **Claude** model family and **Constitutional AI**. The company ships commercial models via claude.ai, the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry, with a strong emphasis on alignment research, red-teaming, and its [[Responsible Scaling Policy]] (ASL) framework.
