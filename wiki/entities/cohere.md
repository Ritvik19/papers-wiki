# Cohere

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Enterprise AI company (founded 2019; co-founders include Aidan Gomez, Ivan Zhang, Nick Frosst) building generative models (**Command** family), retrieval models (**Embed**, **Rerank**), speech (**Transcribe**), and the **North** agent platform. **Cohere For AI** / **Cohere Labs** drives open-science multilingual research through the **Aya** initiative.
