# Aaron Courville

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Aaron Courville is a professor and researcher specializing in deep learning, probabilistic models, and computer vision. He co-authored *Deep Learning* (MIT Press, 2016) with Ian Goodfellow and Yoshua Bengio.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — co-author of the canonical deep learning textbook ingested into this wiki.

## Notes

Courville's expertise in probabilistic graphical models and vision applications informs the book's treatment of structured models, inference, and convolutional architectures.
