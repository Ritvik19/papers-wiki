# PyTorch

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Meta's open-source deep learning framework. Unsloth integrates with PyTorch 2.x APIs including `torch.compile`, [[Flex Attention]], and distributed training primitives; Unsloth joined the official PyTorch Ecosystem in 2025.
