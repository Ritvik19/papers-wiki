# Thinking Machines Lab

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Thinking Machines Lab is an AI research organisation focused on making fine-tuning and post-training more accessible, efficient, and widely applicable. It publishes the "Connectionism" research blog.

## Appearances

- [[Inkling]] — first open-weights multimodal MoE (975B/41B active, 1M context) trained from scratch; released with [[Tinker]] fine-tuning.
- [[Inkling-Small]] — efficient family member (276B/12B active); distilled from Inkling via on-policy distillation.
- [[Tinker]] — managed fine-tuning, distillation, and RL platform for open-weights models.
- [[LoRA Without Regret]] — the lab produced this study characterising the low-regret regime where LoRA matches full fine-tuning for both SFT and RL post-training.
- [[Controlling Reasoning Effort in LLMs]] — Inkling continuous effort conditioning (0–1) via system message + per-token penalty in RL.
