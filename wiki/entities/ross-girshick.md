# Ross Girshick

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Ross Girshick is a computer vision researcher central to the **R-CNN** detector lineage and the proof that **DPMs unroll as CNNs** (CVPR 2015). He led or co-led R-CNN, Fast R-CNN, Faster R-CNN (with Ren/He/Sun), and Mask R-CNN (with He/Gkioxari/Dollár).
