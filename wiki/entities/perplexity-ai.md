# Perplexity AI

**Type**: org

## Overview

AI company building answer engines and APIs for web-grounded search, reasoning models (Sonar family), embedding models (pplx-embed), and agentic retrieval products. Publishes research on search-augmented LLMs, embeddings, speculative inference, and RL training.
