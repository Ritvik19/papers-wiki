# Demis Hassabis

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Sir Demis Hassabis CBE FRS FREng FMedSci is a British computer scientist, AI researcher, and entrepreneur. He is the CEO and co-founder of DeepMind (now [[Google DeepMind]]), the Nobel laureate in Chemistry (2024, shared with John Jumper and David Baker for AlphaFold), and a prominent voice on AGI development timelines and frontier safety governance.

## Appearances

- [[A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age]] — Author of the July 2026 policy essay proposing a FINRA-style public-private Standards Body for testing frontier AI models, mandatory pre-release review, and coordinated development slowdowns.
- [[Gemini 3]] — Led the technical announcement and deployment of Google's flagship Gemini 3 frontier model family.

## Notes

Hassabis's public positions combine strong techno-optimism regarding AGI's potential for scientific discovery (AlphaFold, drug design, materials science, clean energy) with calls for rigorous, institutionalized safety gates. His July 2026 framework advocates moving beyond unilateral corporate commitments toward a formal, federally overseen self-regulatory organization (SRO) for frontier labs.
