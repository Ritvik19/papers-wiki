# Turing

**Type**: org
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Turing is an AI data and evaluation company that partners with model developers to build training data, benchmarks, and evaluation environments. It collaborated with Meta and Hugging Face on OpenEnv, contributing the Calendar Gym, a production-grade environment for evaluating tool-using agents under realistic access-control and multi-step-workflow constraints.

## Appearances

- [[OpenEnv in Practice: Evaluating Tool-Using Agents in Real-World Environments]] — contributes the Calendar Gym environment and co-authors findings on agent reliability under ambiguity and multi-step reasoning demands.
