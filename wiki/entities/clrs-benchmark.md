# CLRS Benchmark

**Type**: entity  
**Tags**: #entity #concept

## Overview
The CLRS Algorithmic Reasoning Benchmark (and its LLM text extension CLRS-Text) developed by DeepMind, covering 30+ classical algorithms from the CLRS textbook to evaluate algorithmic execution and length extrapolation in neural networks and LLMs.

## Appearances
- [[Papers Explained: CLRS and CLRS-Text Benchmark]] — foundational benchmark papers (Veličković et al. 2022, Mirjanić et al. 2024).

## Notes
- Tracks node, edge, and graph probes at every algorithmic execution step.
