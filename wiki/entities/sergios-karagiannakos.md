# Sergios Karagiannakos

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Sergios Karagiannakos is an AI Summer blog author who writes practitioner-oriented deep learning tutorials, including early coverage of autoencoders, regularization, and optimization.

## Appearances

- [[How to Generate Images using Autoencoders]] — 2018 primer on autoencoders, VAEs, and MNIST image generation.
- [[The Theory behind Latent Variable Models: Formulating a Variational Autoencoder]] — 2021 probabilistic derivation of VAEs from latent-variable and variational-inference foundations.
- [[How Diffusion Models Work: The Math from Scratch]] — 2022 co-authored DDPM math tutorial covering forward/reverse diffusion, ELBO training, guided diffusion, and score-based models.
- [[Graph Neural Networks — An Overview]] — 2020 short GNN primer: RNN-on-graphs analogy, synchronous message passing, sum readout.
- [[Best Graph Neural Network Architectures: GCN, GAT, MPNN and More]] — 2021 GNN architecture survey: spectral GCN lineage, MPNN, GAT, GraphSAGE, PinSAGE, Temporal Graph Networks.
- [[Unravel Policy Gradients and REINFORCE]] — 2018 RL primer: policy-based methods, REINFORCE/Monte Carlo policy gradient, Karpathy Pong, CartPole Keras code.
- [[The Idea behind Actor-Critics and How A2C and A3C Improve Them]] — 2018 actor–critic, A2C, and A3C tutorial.
- [[Trust Region and Proximal Policy Optimization (TRPO and PPO)]] — 2019 TRPO trust regions and PPO-Clip introduction.
