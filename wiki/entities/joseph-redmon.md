# Joseph Redmon

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Joseph Redmon is the lead author of the **YOLO** real-time object detection family (YOLO, YOLOv2, YOLO9000, YOLOv3) and co-author of the original unified detection paper (Redmon, Divvala, Girshick, Farhadi, CVPR 2016).

## Appearances

- [[Object Detection Part 4]] — grid formulation, loss design, YOLOv2 improvements (Darknet-19, anchors, WordTree).

## YOLO lineage (blog coverage)

| Version | Notable additions |
|---------|-------------------|
| YOLO v1 | \(S\times S\) grid, \(5B+K\) output, real-time |
| YOLOv2 | BatchNorm, anchors, passthrough, multi-scale |
| YOLO9000 | 9000 classes, hierarchical WordTree |
| YOLOv3 | Multi-scale (speed/mAP figure in post) |
