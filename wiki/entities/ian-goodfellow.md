# Ian Goodfellow

**Type**: person  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Ian Goodfellow is a machine learning researcher and lead author of *Deep Learning* (MIT Press, 2016) with Yoshua Bengio and Aaron Courville. He is widely known for generative adversarial networks (GANs) and work on adversarial examples and generative modeling.

## Appearances

- [[Deep Learning]] — lead author of the canonical deep learning textbook ingested into this wiki.
- [[GANs in Computer Vision: Introduction to Generative Learning]] — original GAN paper (2014) and Improved GAN training tricks (2016) are central references in the AI Summer survey.

## Notes

Goodfellow's contributions span security (adversarial examples), generative modeling, and the pedagogical synthesis in the Deep Learning book. The textbook remains a primary reference despite rapid post-2016 advances in transformers and scaling.
