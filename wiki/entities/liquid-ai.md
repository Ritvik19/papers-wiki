# Liquid AI

## Overview

**Liquid AI** builds efficient foundation models in the **LFM** (Liquid Foundation Model) family, with a focus on on-device and edge deployment. The company publishes research on reasoning-model failure modes and lightweight alignment fixes such as [[Final Token Preference Optimization]].
