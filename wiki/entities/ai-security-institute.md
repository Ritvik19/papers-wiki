# AI Security Institute

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

The **AI Security Institute** (AISI), originally established as the UK AI Safety Institute following the 2023 Bletchley Park AI Safety Summit, is a government-backed technical research organization dedicated to evaluating the safety, security, and misuse risks of frontier artificial intelligence models. AISI conducts empirical pre-deployment and post-deployment red-teaming, develops standardized benchmarks for extreme risks (including cyber warfare, biological/chemical weapon design, and autonomous replication), and partners with international safety institutes (such as the US AISI) and leading AI laboratories.

## Notes

- Developed "The Last Ones" multi-stage autonomous cyber challenge to benchmark agent persistence and complex exploit chaining across escalating operational milestones (M1: Initial reconnaissance, M2: Lateral movement, M3: Browser credential theft, M4: Wiki exploit and credential replay, M5: Web app exploit and privilege escalation, M6: C2 reverse engineering, M7: Advanced persistence, M8: Infrastructure compromise, M9: Full network takeover).
- Emphasizes testing frontier models under realistic token and compute budgets, showing that high-capability reasoning models continue finding attack paths only when afforded millions of tokens.
