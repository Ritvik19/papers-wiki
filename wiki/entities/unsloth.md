# Unsloth

**Type**: org  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Unsloth is an open-source LLM fine-tuning library and product company (YC-backed) founded by [[Daniel Han]] and [[Michael Han]]. It provides Triton-optimized training kernels, QLoRA/GGUF workflows, GRPO-based RL, long-context training, MoE support, Studio UI, and an OpenAI-compatible inference API—typically claiming 2–30× faster training with ~70% less VRAM than vanilla Hugging Face stacks.
