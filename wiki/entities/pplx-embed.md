# pplx-embed

**Type**: tool  
**Tags**: #entity

## Overview

Perplexity's family of multilingual text embedding models for web-scale retrieval: **pplx-embed-v1** (standard dense retrieval) and **pplx-embed-context-v1** (contextualized passage embeddings), each at 0.6B and 4B scales with native INT8/binary outputs.
